# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical evaluators: senior engineers, technical founders, and hiring leads who review portfolios critically. They read the work section. They arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, or direct referrals. They spend 90 seconds deciding whether to keep reading.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio that functions as the definitive professional record for a systems builder who bridges AI, infrastructure, and operations. Success means a visitor finishes reading and either reaches out directly or sends the link to someone who should.

## Brand Personality

Precise, formidable, lived-in. Not a startup founder's personal brand. Not a junior developer's showcase. Someone who has built real systems under real constraints and has the depth and the craft to prove it. The name "Aime Cesaire" carries weight — a Rwandan engineer named after a Martinican poet of the Negritude movement. That depth should show.

## Anti-references

- Generic "dark indigo developer portfolio" — the Tailwind starter kit aesthetic that ships with periwinkle accents
- Cards-on-white React scroll template — identical icon-heading-text grids repeated endlessly
- SaaS landing page affectations: gradient text, hero metrics, glassmorphism panels
- Editorial-magazine dark with display serif italics and rule-separated columns (second-order reflex — avoids indigo but lands in the next trap)

## Design Principles

1. Show the work, not the wrapper — the projects carry the weight; the design surfaces them without competing.
2. Depth rewards patience — someone who reads carefully finds more than someone who scans.
3. Craft proves the claim — if this person claims to care about design, the portfolio itself must demonstrate that.
4. Personal history is an asset — the Rwanda origin story is rare and should be surfaced, not buried.
5. No hedge words — every heading and label should be direct and confident.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Sufficient contrast for all text against dark backgrounds.
